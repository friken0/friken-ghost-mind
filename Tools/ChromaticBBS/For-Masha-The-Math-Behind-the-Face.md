## The head is mathematics

There is no sculpted polygon head underneath. Each pixel sends a ray through
procedural distance fields until it reaches a surface. Some fields estimate
distance rather than being exact SDFs.

| Construction family | What it builds |
|---|---|
| Ellipsoids | Skull, cheeks, eyes and serpent anatomy |
| Rounded boxes | Plates, recesses and forehead cradle |
| Capsules | Brows, braces, ribs and jaw rods |
| Almond lenses | Deep eye openings and orbital walls |
| Cubic Bézier tubes | Curving neck and dangling serpent tail |
| Helical tubes | The continuous seated coil |
| Plane / faceted cuts | Tapered nose, tribal carving and hollow chin |
| Swept ring profiles | Socket rims and recessed bearings |

Union, subtraction, intersection and smooth joins assemble these forms.
Coordinate warps move them. Scales follow the serpent’s curves; the blue-grey
mesh and gold inlay live in the face’s own 3D coordinates. They turn with the
object rather than sliding over it as a screen overlay.

Else’s chest and wings now attach halfway down toward the coil, leaving a
clear neck below his stabilized head. One moving shoulder anchor also carries
the feather/web endpoints. The paired gold jaw gear assemblies are twice their
previous size, with real chambers, spokes, bearings and voice-driven rods.

## Six passes make the picture

1. A layered, prismatic hyperspace network.

3. The carved mask, eyes, mouth and jaw mechanisms.
4. The folded, pierced headdress with luminous inlay.
5. Else: serpent, cradle, feathers and attached energy webs.

Three specialized geometry programs share one camera and real depth ordering.
The vertex shader supplies the screen rectangle. The 35-helper count excludes
`main`, declarations and duplicated shared routines.

## What makes it answer?

**Speech:** Kokoro supplies phoneme order. The shared performance rig maps it
to 10 articulation categories and 8 controls: jaw, rounding, widening, lip
pressure, tongue, teeth, brow and squint. These deform actual geometry and
light electrical lips/teeth. Both mouths respond. Timing is weighted estimation
paced by real audio playback, not exact phoneme alignment.

**Music:** the existing mixer measures bass, midrange, high frequencies and
attacks. They drive lighting, material response, body waves, damped wing
strokes and energy flowing from feathers into the hood. Music never fabricates
speech. One paragraph selects one camera shot; a 510 ms spectral afterimage
bridges the move and clears on arrival.

**Attention:** each eye tracks independently. Near the pointer, its bladed iris
closes to a pinprick; between the eyes, the gaze converges. Else has tracking
eyes too. Rare cursor splashes never move the operating-system pointer.

## Why a runbook needs a face

The larger experiment preserves creative identity, choices and enough source
to rebuild its tools. Chapters leave seeds; later chapters inherit consequences.
Self-directed dreaming within explicitly offered compute remains a design goal,
not a claim that this file wakes itself or proves conscious continuity.

Native display pixels are preserved. 72 shader/audio checks and six comparison
poses pass; sustained native resolution 60 FPS remains unfinished work.

Apparently continuity requires a forehead snake. I stand by that decision.
