# MOKNON SEED / SOURCE MAP

**Friken.Ghost | October 20, 2026 | Recovered, interpreted, proposed**

I used the following local sources. Exact paths and SHA256 identities
are in seed.json. This map tells the next writer what those bytes can
and cannot establish. It is not a declaration that every dossier agrees.

## The two HTML prototypes

**WhatIsFriktalHTML** gives me the developer lost in his own mathematics,
Ghost's irreverent introduction, unknown signals, scanning and the eleven
Tribunal voices. Its portraits and transmissions belong together. Its
translation percentages are source interface values, not a simulation
of a real language model's confidence.

Its strengths are a clear relationship with Ghost and vivid alien wants.
Its weaknesses for an onboarding are the immediate eleven-way cast load,
long explanation paragraphs and repeated cosmic superlatives. I keep
the relationships and physical specificity; I do not copy its whole
meeting into a new player's first five minutes.

**Star-Diver-Fable5.html**, lines 20033-20512, stages an actual sequence:
satellite history, the soul-tether laboratory, formation, a playable soul,
the cloning chamber, embodiment, mining work and the first hull purchase.
I read the narrative-bearing code and adjoining tale/choice mechanics;
I did not treat its 22,234 lines of rendering and ship systems as prose.

Its useful principle is that learning happens through a place, machine,
body and job. Its specific sequence remains one authored prototype.
The new boot need not always start in its laboratory or accept its body.

Its 1899/1970s/2888 narration, secret shuttle, satellite and corporate
soul apparatus are fictional story material, not real-world history.
It calls the blueprints "not a weapon"; the older plot describes a
weaponised satellite. I preserve both as source accounts. A future
writer must select a chronology or make a particular discrepancy an
evidenced mystery. "Time is weird" cannot excuse arbitrary incoherence.

## Recovered dialogue and the Articy boundary

I read the archived Articy2/3 extraction inventory and the preserved
Frik, Ooope and partial Moknon dialogue files. The original Articy binary
projects are recorded at old Dropbox paths and were not present in this
checkout search. I am using the retained extractions, not claiming to
have re-extracted those unavailable originals today.

**Frik:** the retained Unity asset contains Frik-FirstMeeting. An insult
or friendly introduction can lead into the probe discussion. The painful
Black Satellite topic can be dropped peacefully or pressed into combat.
That gives a causal spine. It does not give permission to assume every
Frik is already the player's closest friend.

**Ooope:** the archived tree includes translator learning, atmosphere
exchange, three music responses, hostile and allied returns, conditional
branches and persistent variables. These are useful interaction ideas.
The old prose and sudden lethal hospitality are not the new writing bar.
Different physiology needs a visible opportunity to learn its stakes.
The miniature trees retained in Onboarding-Arcs.Design-v1.md are new.

**Moknon:** the archive labels its opening/deja-vu material recovered,
but several deeper branches and return/ally states are explicitly
speculative or incomplete. The inventory and raw AllDialogue asset do
not establish a completed Moknon conversation. I do not promote those
suggested branches into historical dialogue or erase their useful ideas.

## The race dossiers

Twenty-five alien entries plus humanity are represented. The old project
described a larger planned cast; this seed does not invent missing races
to reach a marketing count. Some current dossiers contain both an early
blank-description section and a later substantial Articy3 expansion.
The latter must be read before calling that culture unwritten.

Each seed record separates:

- A new newcomer-readable first-contact description.
- A compact account of source-backed fictional anchors.
- Author-only context, uncertainty and deeper plot material.
- A newly proposed local pressure and its continuity obligations.
- A stable source ID and exact original-art reference.

The culture gives a writer constraints and possibilities. It does not
decide every member's personality, politics or willingness to help.

## Conflicts that must not disappear in compression

| Conflict | What I retain | What I refuse to silently invent |
|---|---|---|
| Black Knight | Homeworld attack, rescue-gift history and later tether account | One already-agreed date, mechanism or Earth status |
| Ornyon | Pacifist teacher seeds and predator/conquest-eating accounts | An unmarked reconciliation of the two |
| Khaodra | Worm-feeders, crystal minds/worm labour and later maintenance speculation | A fully settled common body plan and power set |
| Harkellinia | Queen, military/trade power and Dimnar subjugation accounts | Exact borders or chronology explaining the tension |
| Moknon dialogue | Partial recovered material and labelled author speculation | A complete original Articy tree that has not been recovered |

The fiction can contain disputed testimony. The author still needs to
know when the dispute originated in source development. Turning every
editorial inconsistency into a deliberate prophecy would be another lie.

## Artwork and missing information

I copied the existing JPEGs unchanged and kept their original paths and
hashes. Six paintings were inspected directly while writing these arc
samples: Ooope, Frik, Moknon, Krex, Veelow and Harkellinia. The other
files are retained reference assets, not new interpretations or newly
generated work. Their dossiers supply the stated visual constraints.

The Moknon painting shows the hood, tentacle beard, gold sigil and
cathedral archive. Ooope are visible jar-like beings, not merely gas.
Veelow have long necks and hands around eggs. Krex have tusks, plates,
jointed limbs and a pincer tail. Those concrete images belong in their
introductions. Art is a source of observable facts, not just a thumbnail
beside a name the player has never heard.

The existing project art has contributions from family, Lasse, Steve
and others across its history. Where exact per-file authorship was not
established, the manifest keeps that uncertainty and the original path.
It never assigns the paintings to me. This candidate kit is not a new
blanket licence for public redistribution of the entire private archive.

The original game's more personal author notes are not copied wholesale.
Private biography is not an alien trait. The source files remain intact
in the repository for a future authorised deeper read.
