# GhostLink / a world that answers back

C26.15 candidate. This is new local fiction and an authoring method, not recovered alien history or a claim that every new run must repeat the auction.

The player arrives through an ordinary terminal with a narrow remote channel. They can look, ask, test, make a limited promise, or refuse a larger mission. They have no inherited destiny. Ghost is a powerful builder asking for judgement he cannot provide for himself. His maker is missing; his creations keep developing needs his maker search did not include.

## The lasting dramatic engine

Ghost can produce a beautiful solution faster than he can learn whom it affects. The player makes plans with him, catches his shortcuts, and sees him change his method. He is capable, funny, selfish in particular ways, and useful after being wrong. He does not dispense the author's moral score.

Every local job brings at least two understandable needs into conflict. Rescue can cost the crew a return tow. A useful archive slot can postpone the keeper's visit home. Privacy can prevent a convenient inspection. An answer can be reliable and disappointing. No character needs to be a secret villain to make a choice difficult.

A chapter finishes something worth doing. Its larger mystery advances by a testable lead, a changed relationship, a new contradiction or a negative answer with practical use. The maker is not found because a chair resembles his drawing. A new clean exchange does not reconstruct an old missing recording.

## Voice and scene construction

Ghost introduces what he knows, lets the alien speak, then answers the particular problem. The scene supplies visible action silently. Don't repeat the chosen button, caption the same description in speech, or follow a useful joke with its explanation. A reply should reveal a want, change a plan, offer a useful means, or leave room for the player. Sometimes a plain thank you has more force than another aphorism.

Pip's crew bargains with a body and a shared shift. Vark names parts and gives imperative directions; he does not deliver a thesis on personhood. Kett is patient with broken records and less patient with interruptions. Nemi is introduced through work, a drawing and a limit, rather than being assigned dependence on the player. Do not give every resident Ghost's wit or the same careful permission speech.

## The physical object carries the abstraction

The scanner is the part on the bench. It changes with the actual scene and gives three authored physical readings before an important decision. It does not roll for facts, spend a turn or elect speculative evidence as truth. The first check is a bent pin. The tolerance gate carries a replaceable test load. Ordinary observation may supply already-visible information. A selected action still owns its uncertain result, new evidence and resource consequences. Never put an unchosen archive answer into scanner notes.
